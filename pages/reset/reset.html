Reset protection?

Resetting protection will remove your current authentication settings. For your security, Chrome Profile Guard cannot recover a forgotten PIN or password — resetting is the only way to regain access.

This will permanently:
  • delete your PIN or password
  • remove your protected-website list
  • clear your settings and local activity log
You'll need to complete setup again.
